# Join the outcome-neutral pilot

CounterSignal is recruiting adults in CALL-E-supported calling regions who genuinely work for a
small or midsize commercial contractor and directly manage municipal permit follow-up.

The pilot is one short, AI-disclosed phone interview using the five questions and 8/5/3 decision
rule already frozen in the repository. It is not a sales call. The agent does not ask for a name,
project address, permit number, credentials, payment data, or another phone number. A participant
may decline or end the call at any time.

## What counts

- A confirmation and a contradiction pass the same consent, segment, grounding and binding gates.
- Ambiguous answers, refusal, voicemail and wrong-segment contacts do not enter the completed
  denominator and are not blindly redialed.
- A technical cross-tester who does not match the contractor segment can help validate transport,
  but is never represented as customer-discovery evidence.
- One completed interview is directional evidence, not a representative sample or a
  product-market-fit claim. The frozen target is eight completed interviews.

## Privacy and consent

Public proof may include a CALL-E call ID, terminal status, classification and redacted decision.
It excludes the participant's phone number, identity, transcript, recording and verbatim quote.
Publishing any quote would require separate, explicit permission after the interview.

To volunteer, use **Message manager** on the
[CounterSignal Devpost page](https://devpost.com/software/countersignal). Do not post a phone
number in a public issue or discussion. The exact protocol and publication boundary will be shared
before a call is scheduled.
